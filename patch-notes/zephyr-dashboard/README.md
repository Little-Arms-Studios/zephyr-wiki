# Zephyr Dashboard

## v2026.6.0

#### August 20, 2026

#### Highlights

* Partner program - Partners now have their own dashboard, referral tracking, and shareable product checkout links. Admins can manage partners and commissions from the Manage tables.
* Shop publishing - Products can be shown or hidden in the shop independently of whether they are active.

***

#### New Features

* Partner accounts - Create and edit partners, including referral codes and completed-referral counts.
* Partner dashboard - Partners can see completed referrals, this month’s commission, pending payout, and paid year-to-date, plus a monthly referrals breakdown.
* Partner referrals - Admins can manage referrals.
* Partner product checkout - Share a product link with a partner referral code (`/partner-checkout`). Buyers land on the product and continue into checkout with the referral attached.
* In Shop - Mark products to display in the shop. Publish or unpublish from the products table, or use the toggle when creating or editing a product.

***

#### Improvements

* Reseller terminology is now Partner throughout the dashboard and related flows.
* Products table includes an In Shop column and Publish to Shop / Unpublish from Shop actions.
* Shop listings no longer include certification products, cart errors name the product involved, and Add to Cart copy reflects whether you already own the item.
* Updated the Omada logo in the Proud Partners section.

***

#### Bug Fixes

* Modules table - Opening the Modules table no longer fails to load.
* License expiration - The expiration field now saves correctly when creating and editing products.
