# ⭐ v2026.6.2

### **September 1, 2026**

**Highlights**

* Search and discovery improvements across public pages (sitemap, robots, and canonical URLs), a blank-page loading fix, and richer invite and partner-referral detail views in the dashboard.

***

**New Features**

* Invite details - Click an invite row to open a detail view with status, lifecycle steps, and actions such as copy link, resend, and delete.
* Partner referral timeline - Partner referrals show a status history timeline in the detail view, with a hover preview on the referrals table.

***

**Improvements**

* SEO - Public pages now include sitemap and robots support, clearer page metadata, and self-referencing canonical URLs for indexable routes.
* Manage tables - Several tables (including Invites, Organizations, Partners, Products, and Users) support clicking a row to open the related detail view, with clearer row hover feedback.
* Site map page - The on-site Site Map view was updated to align with the new public sitemap configuration.

***

**Bug Fixes**

* Blank page - Fixed an issue where some pages could render as a white/blank screen.
* Invite viewing - Viewing invite details is limited to admins for now.
